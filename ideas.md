# [IAN] Client Portal — Design Direction

## Direction: Quiet confidence
A premium, editorial client workspace that feels like a considered studio notebook rather than a traditional SaaS dashboard.

- **Movement:** warm minimalism with an editorial rhythm.
- **Core principles:** calm hierarchy, clear next actions, generous breathing room, human language.
- **Colour philosophy:** ink and warm paper as the foundation, with a confident coral accent for action and a cool blue signal for progress.
- **Layout paradigm:** mobile-first stacked cards that widen into a two-column editorial canvas on desktop.
- **Signature elements:** oversized phase numbers, thin rules, rounded paper cards, a persistent left rail on desktop, sticky action row on mobile.
- **Interaction philosophy:** one obvious next step per surface; progress is visible without feeling gamified.
- **Typography:** system sans for utility; strong serif display moments for warmth and studio character.
- **Brand essence:** INNOVATE · ARCHITECT · NAVIGATE.
- **Brand voice:** personal, direct, reassuring, non-technical.
- **Wordmark:** [IAN] in a compact bracketed lockup.
- **Signature colour:** coral #ee745f.
